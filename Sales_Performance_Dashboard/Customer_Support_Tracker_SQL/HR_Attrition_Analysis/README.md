# HR Attrition Analysis — Power BI

## Project Overview
This project analyzes employee attrition to understand why employees leave, which groups are most affected, and what factors contribute to turnover trends.

## Business Problem
HR needed insights into:
Departments with high attrition
Age groups most likely to leave
Salary and job role impact on attrition
Overall employee satisfaction patterns

## Tools Used
Excel
Power BI

## Steps Followed
1. Cleaned HR dataset by removing blanks and correcting category formats.
2. Imported the cleaned file into Power BI.
3. Created measures for attrition rate, employee count, and average monthly income.
4. Built visualizations for attrition by department, age group, gender, and job role.

## Key Insights
Highest attrition was seen in the 25 to 34 age group.
Certain job roles showed more turnover due to workload and low pay.
Departments with low satisfaction scores had higher exit rates.
Higher income levels generally had lower attrition.

## Project Outcome
This project shows ability to:
Clean and prepare HR data
Build interactive dashboards in Power BI
Analyze employee patterns and risk factors
Provide clear conclusions for business decisions
